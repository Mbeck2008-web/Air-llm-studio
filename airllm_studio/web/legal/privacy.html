AirLLM Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

AirLLM Studio runs large language models on your Mac. This policy describes what the app stores and what leaves the device.

Data stored on your Mac

  • Chat transcripts, settings, and In-App Purchase receipts in Application Support.
  • Model weights you choose to download (not bundled with the app).
  • An optional Hugging Face token, stored locally, used only to fetch gated models you request.

Network

  • Hugging Face, only when you add, search, or prepare a model.
  • Optional web search (a Pro feature), only when you enable tools and tick Search on a message.
  • Apple In-App Purchase for paid unlocks.

What we do not collect

The app does not include analytics, advertising, or account sign-in of its own. We do not operate a cloud inference service. Prompts stay on your Mac unless you turn on web search for that message.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your Apple ID password or full payment details. Restore Purchases re-reads prior App Store transactions (or the local test store when StoreKit is unavailable).
