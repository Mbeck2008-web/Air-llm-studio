The free tier

You may add, prepare, and load local models and send chats (including the demo/mock engine when machine-learning dependencies are not installed) without paying.

Paid unlocks

Pro extras — web search / tool calling, higher token limits, and Hugging Face library search — unlock only through Apple In-App Purchase. License keys and third-party payment codes are not accepted.

Subscriptions

Pro Monthly auto-renews every month. Pro Yearly auto-renews every year. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation. Subscriptions renew unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in System Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Pro Lifetime is a one-time non-consumable purchase and does not auto-renew.
